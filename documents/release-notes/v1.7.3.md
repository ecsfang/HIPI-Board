# HIPI 1.7.3

TFI2C can now measure the Bosch pressure and humidity sensors that the I2C
chip list used to find but not read: BMP390/BMP388, BMP180/BMP085, BME280 and
BME680/BME688.

## Downloads

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `hipi_7_pico-v1.7.3.uf2` | The firmware for the 7" panel — drag it onto the Pico in BOOTSEL mode |
| `HIPI-SD-card-v1.7.3.zip` | Everything a new SD card needs — unzip it onto the card |
| `hipi_7_pico-v1.7.3.elf` | The firmware with debug symbols, for developers |

## New

- **BMP390 and BMP388** (TFI2C `MT`/`MP`): temperature and pressure, measured
  with the chip's own calibration. Chip ID 0x60 (BMP390) or 0x50 (BMP388) in
  register 0x00.
- **BMP180 and BMP085** (TFI2C `MT`/`MP`): temperature and pressure. Both have
  the chip ID 0x55 and are calculated the same way. They were found before,
  but answered `ERR NOACK` when measured.
- **BME280** (TFI2C `MT`/`MP`/`MH`): temperature, pressure and humidity.
- **BME680 and BME688** (TFI2C `MT`/`MP`/`MH`): temperature, pressure and
  humidity. The gas sensor is not read.

All four are in the chip list built into the firmware, with their own entries
in `chips.txt` and their own `bmp390.*`, `bmp180.*`, `bme280.*` and
`bme680.*` measurements.

## Fixed

- A BMP180/BMP085 (0x77) and a BME680 (0x61) were identified on the Hardware
  test page but gave `ERR NOACK` on `MT`/`MP`: the measurement code only knew
  the BMP280. Each chip now has its own measurement.

## Upgrading

- Flash `hipi_7_pico-v1.7.3.uf2` as usual. Your `CONFIG.TXT` keeps working.
- The SD card doesn't need to change. The chip list is built into the
  firmware; `i2c/chips.txt.example` on the card is only a reference.
- Not yet tried on real hardware: BME280 (the formulas are tested on the PC
  against the datasheet's values).
