# HIPI 1.3.0

This release makes the plotter a far more complete **HP 7470A**: user units,
circles and arcs, line types, character sets and digitizing with a panel on
the screen. There's also support for the **PCF85063A** clock chip, a reworked
HP-IL device list, saved printouts that can be loaded again, and a number of
fixes.

## Downloads

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `hipi_7_pico-v1.3.0.uf2` | The firmware for the 7" panel — drag it onto the Pico in BOOTSEL mode |
| `HIPI-SD-card-v1.3.0.zip` | Everything a new SD card needs — unzip it onto the card |
| `hipi_7_pico-v1.3.0.elf` | The firmware with debug symbols, for developers |

## New

### Plotter (HP 7470A)

- **User units:** `IP` (P1/P2) and `SC` (scaling). Text size set with `SR`
  follows a new P1/P2.
- **Circles and arcs:** `CI`, `AA`, `AR`.
- **Line types** `LT 0–6` with pattern length, as on the 7470A.
- **Symbol mode** `SM`, relative label direction `DR`, `DF`.
- **Character sets 0–4** (`CS`, `CA`, `SS`, `SA`, SO/SI in labels): ASCII,
  HP 9825, French/German, Scandinavian, Spanish. Accents are drawn over the
  character before them — `CS3;LB35-50 A|R` gives *35-50 ÅR*.
- **Digitizing** (`DP`, `OD`, `DC`): the Plotter view shows a panel with a
  loupe, X/Y, arrows that move the marker a pixel, ENTER and X. Touch the
  plot, adjust, press ENTER.
- **Status and errors** as on the real plotter: `OS` bits 1, 2 and 5, `OE`
  error codes 1–3.
- **Labels and `CP`** work like the 7470A (character cells, carriage-return
  point), checked against pyILPER's plotter engine.
- Commands HIPI doesn't know are written once to the USB log, also without
  trace.

### Clock

- The **PCF85063A** real-time clock (I2C 0x51) is found by itself, next to
  the DS3231/DS1307. It's shown on the Hardware test page, and TFI2C can
  read its time and date.

### Views and touch

- **HP-IL device list:** bigger text, a touch on a device's box switches it
  on or off, swipe up/down to scroll.
- **Loop map:** talkers and listeners in their own colours, and a
  **GO TO … VIEW** button in a device's details.
- **Printer:** **Printouts** in its menu lists the saved printouts, which
  can be loaded back into the printer.
- **Demo:** now also shows the media map, the clock, tearing off paper and
  the HP-IL signals view.

## Fixed

- Plotter: commands split over two lines (CR LF inside a number) lost the
  rest of the line; text left of P1 lost its first letter; `CP-9.-1` was read
  as one number.
- Touch stopped working after the screen saver if the device list had been
  open.
- The menu could switch to a larger font when scrolling in Devices / On-Off.
- Tape view: REWIND now also moves the head in the media map; the media map
  is redrawn after formatting.
- Only LED4 shows HP-IL activity; *fault* on the Hardware test page is
  magenta.

## Upgrading

- Flash `hipi_7_pico-v1.3.0.uf2` as usual. Your `CONFIG.TXT` keeps working.
- The SD card doesn't need to change.
